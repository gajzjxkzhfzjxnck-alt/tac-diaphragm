Our Story

Senioracoustic (Guangdong Tianbo Technology Co., Ltd.) is an audio technology company headquartered in Dongguan, Guangdong, specializing in the R&D and production of high-end speaker diaphragms and audio test equipment.

With years of experience in audio test equipment R&D and production, we have independently developed our own analysis software system. We operate a full-anechoic chamber and are equipped with audio analyzers, electroacoustic testers, Bluetooth analyzers, artificial mouths, artificial ears, artificial heads, and other professional test equipment.

Our Philosophy

We believe that the diaphragm is the heart of any speaker. An ideal diaphragm must simultaneously achieve light weight, high Young's modulus, appropriate damping, and minimal split vibration. The key lies in the balance of transient response — vibrating immediately upon receiving the signal and stopping promptly when the signal ceases.

"The ta-C diamond diaphragm achieves a perfect balance between sound conduction velocity and internal resistance — delivering ultra-high sensitivity and exceptional transient response for accurate sound reproduction."
