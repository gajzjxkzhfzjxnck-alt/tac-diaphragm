Cleer ARC 6 Music Edition

ta-C Diamond-Coated Diaphragm in a Flagship Open-Ear Headphone

Overview

Cleer, a premium audio brand under Grandsun Electronics, launched the ARC 6 Music Edition — an open-ear true wireless headphone featuring Tianbo's ta-C (DLC) diamond-coated composite diaphragm as a key acoustic component.

The ARC 6 Music Edition is the upgraded variant of the standard ARC 6. While the standard version uses a magnesium-lithium alloy diaphragm, the Music Edition upgrades to a 16.2mm DLC/ta-C coated composite diaphragm, delivering significantly improved high-frequency extension and lower distortion.

Why ta-C for Open-Ear Headphones?

The technical challenges of open-ear designs make ta-C the ideal diaphragm choice

🎯

Compensating for Bass Limitations

Open-ear designs naturally lack bass isolation. The ta-C coating's high rigidity enables more precise low-frequency control, maximizing what the open-ear form factor can deliver.

📊

Low Distortion at High Volumes

At higher SPLs where standard diaphragms begin to distort, ta-C's enhanced rigidity maintains piston-like behavior, keeping THD exceptionally low even at maximum output.

💧

Weather Resistance

Open-ear headphones are exposed to the elements. ta-C coating's environmental stability protects the diaphragm from humidity, temperature swings, and UV exposure.
