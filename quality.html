Testing Facilities

We operate a large full-anechoic chamber providing a professional environment for acoustic testing of diaphragms and speaker products. Our testing equipment includes audio analyzers, electroacoustic testers, Bluetooth analyzers, artificial mouths, artificial ears, and artificial heads, along with our proprietary analysis software.
