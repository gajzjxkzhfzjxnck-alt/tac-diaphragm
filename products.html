ta-C Diamond-Coated Diaphragm Products

Diamond-coated diaphragm solutions for different application scenarios

PET + ta-C

PET + ta-C Diamond Coated Diaphragm

PET (Polyester) is one of the most widely used diaphragm materials — lightweight, flexible, with high tensile strength. The ta-C coating addresses PET's stiffness不足 at high frequencies, delivering more accurate and clearer high-frequency reproduction.

Base Material
PET (Polyester)
Coating
ta-C (SP³ >85%)
Thickness Range
0.3 - 1.0 μm
Typical Dia. Range
6 - 50 mm

Applications: High-end IEMs, TWS earbuds, consumer speakers

PEEK + ta-C

PEEK + ta-C Diamond Coated Diaphragm

PEEK (Polyether Ether Ketone) is a high-end diaphragm material known for its high rigidity, lightweight, and thermal stability. The ta-C coating further enhances PEEK's mechanical properties, making it ideal for high-fidelity headphones and professional monitoring.

Base Material
PEEK
Coating
ta-C (SP³ >85%)
Thickness Range
0.3 - 1.0 μm
Typical Dia. Range
6 - 80 mm

Applications: Hi-Fi headphones, studio monitors, automotive audio

PEI + ta-C

PEI + ta-C Diamond Coated Diaphragm

PEI (Polyetherimide) offers excellent mechanical strength, high-temperature resistance, and electrical insulation. The ta-C coating enhances heat dissipation and creep resistance, making it suitable for speakers requiring sustained high-power operation.

Base Material
PEI
Coating
ta-C (SP³ >85%)
Thickness Range
0.3 - 1.0 μm
Typical Dia. Range
10 - 100 mm

Applications: Speaker tweeters, professional audio, automotive audio
