High-End Headphones & IEMs

In headphones and In-Ear Monitors (IEMs), ta-C coated diaphragms deliver ultra-low distortion and exceptional high-frequency extension. The small diaphragm size makes the coating effect particularly pronounced. Already verified in production products (Cleer ARC6 Music Edition).

  • ◆ THD as low as 0.03%
  • ◆ Extended frequency response beyond 40kHz
  • ◆ Superior transient response for detailed sound reproduction
  • ◆ Ideal for dynamic drivers from 6mm to 16mm

Speaker Systems

For tweeters and mid-range drivers, ta-C coated diaphragms push the breakup frequency far above the audible range, enabling pure high-frequency reproduction without complex crossover compensation. The coating significantly improves the performance of aluminum, titanium, and polymer dome diaphragms.

  • ◆ Breakup frequency >80 kHz achievable with proper design
  • ◆ Ideal for dome diameters from 20mm to 100mm
  • ◆ Eliminates need for notch filters in crossover networks
  • ◆ Compatible with aluminum and titanium dome substrates

  • ◆ Resistance to humidity and UV degradation
