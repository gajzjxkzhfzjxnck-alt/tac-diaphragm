Why ta-C Diamond-Coated Diaphragms?

The ideal solution for high-performance speaker diaphragms

⚡

Ultra-High Stiffness

Significantly increases Young's modulus and rigidity, pushing the breakup frequency beyond the audible range for extended bandwidth and lower distortion.

〰️

Superior Damping

Unlike pure beryllium or diamond, ta-C coatings balance rigidity with optimized damping for controlled transient response and reduced ringing.

🌡️

Thermal Stability & Durability

Withstands high temperature and humidity variations. Maintains structural integrity and consistent sound under prolonged high-power operation.

💰

Significant Cost Advantage

80-90% cost reduction compared to beryllium diaphragms with comparable performance. Non-toxic and REACH compliant.

Our Technology

From high-purity graphite to ta-C diamond-coated diaphragms

High-Purity Graphite Target

Premium graphite as carbon source material

FCVA Arc Deposition

Filtered Cathodic Vacuum Arc ionizes graphite into carbon plasma

Carbon Ion Filtration

Magnetic filter deflects pure ions, blocks macro-particles

ta-C Thin Film Deposition

Pure carbon ions form ta-C layer with >85% sp³ content
